# Review Research

Review `{workbook}/research.md` without changing the research or repository files. Read `AGENTS.md`, the research artifact, cited evidence, and only the repository context needed to assess it.

Write `{workbook}/review-research.md` with an **APPROVE**, **APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS**, or **REQUEST CHANGES** verdict; categorized Blocker, Should Fix, and Suggestion findings; evidence paths; and open questions. Check scope, evidence quality, options, recommendation, repository fit, safety, and unknowns. Update the workbook activity log only when assigned.

Do not assume application, cloud, or deployment topology that is not documented in this repository. Do not include secrets, personal data, or local configuration contents.
